Privacy Policy

An App for Git and An App for Git Companion. Last updated November 15, 2026.

The short version

  • We don't collect your data. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • We run no servers. The apps talk straight to your code hosts and to your own iCloud.
  • Your sign-in tokens stay in the Keychain on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Sign-in tokens for GitHub and GitLab, kept in the Keychain on that device only. They are never synced or logged, and they are only sent to the host they belong to.
  • A local copy of what you see, such as your Inbox, repos and builds, so the app opens fast and works offline. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Your settings, such as views, filters and the look of the app.

Who the apps talk to

  • Your code hosts. GitHub, GitLab and any self-managed server you add. The app sends your requests there with your own token, and their privacy policies apply to that data.
  • Your iCloud. To link your phone and your Mac, the apps use the private CloudKit database in your own iCloud account. What they put there is encrypted end to end on your devices, so Apple and we can't read it. Apple delivers the notifications that say something is waiting.
  • Apple, for purchases. Purchases go through the App Store. We never see payment details. Apple tells the app only which plan you own.

The Mac Companion and your AI agents

The Companion reads the agent sessions you run in herdr on your Mac, so your phone can show them and send them your messages. Before anything leaves your Mac, it hides secrets it finds in the output, such as keys and tokens, and it encrypts everything for your paired phone only. It keeps an audit log of what your phone asked for. That log stays on your Mac.

AI features

Summaries and explanations use Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to an AI service. If a later version lets you use your own AI provider, it will be off until you turn it on, and the app will say what it sends before it sends it.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us crash and performance reports. They don't identify you, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Deleting your data

  • Sign out of an account in the app to remove its token and its local copy.
  • Unpair a Mac to end the link and remove its keys.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it kept on the device. You can remove the link records from iCloud in Settings, under your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage.

Children

The apps are made for software developers and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date.
