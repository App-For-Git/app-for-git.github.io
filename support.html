Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/App-For-Git/app-for-git.github.io/issues. Please leave out tokens, private repo names and anything else secret.

Signing in

GitHub uses a short code you enter on github.com. GitLab and self-managed servers can use a personal access token. Tokens stay in the Keychain on your device.

Pairing your Mac

  1. Install An App for Git Companion on your Mac and open it from the menu bar.
  2. Choose Pair a phone. A code appears.
  3. On your phone, go to You, then Mac helper, then Pair, and scan the code.

The Agents tab needs herdr running on your Mac.

Plans

The free plan shows 3 agents at once on 1 Mac. Up to 10 Agents shows up to 10 agents on 2 Macs. Unlimited Agents shows every agent on up to 8 Macs. Each is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Use Restore Purchases on a new device.
